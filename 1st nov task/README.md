## Key Scrum concepts covered

- **Scrum roles:** Product Owner, Scrum Master and Developers
- **Events:** Sprint, Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective
- **Artifacts:** Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog and Increment
- **Commitments:** Product Goal, Sprint Goal and Definition of Done
- **Estimation:** Relative sizing with Fibonacci story points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13); story points are not hours
- **Workflow:** To Do → In Progress → In Review → Done
